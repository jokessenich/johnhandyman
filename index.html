About

Hi, I'm John.

Before I started taking on handyman work in Ann Arbor, I built a tiny home from the ground up — framing, roofing, the whole thing — and fabricated a custom trailer along the way. Projects like that teach you to slow down, measure twice, and finish what you start.

So when someone calls about a sticky door or a wobbly toilet, it's a quick visit, not a project. No upsells, no jargon, no surprises on the invoice. Just the work, done right.

— John Kessenich, Ann Arbor
Specialty

Kids' play areas, built to last.

Most playhouses and swing sets you can buy come from a box, get assembled in an afternoon, and start wobbling within a year. I build them the way a carpenter would — with real lumber, real joinery, and real attention to how kids actually use them.

Whether it's a simple sandbox, a backyard playhouse, or a multi-level climbing setup, I'll work with you on the design and build it to handle a decade of weather, growing kids, and the neighborhood pile-on.

  • Playhouses · custom sized to your yard

  • Swing sets & sandboxes · built in place
